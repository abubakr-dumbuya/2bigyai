Bring Joloff to Your Event

From intimate dinners to corporate events and celebrations, we bring 30 years of authentic Senegalese cuisine to your table. Tell us about your event and we'll craft the perfect experience.

Who we cater for

Every Occasion, Every Table

01
Corporate Events
Team lunches, client dinners, office celebrations, and company retreats. West African cuisine stands out from the sea of Italian and sushi — memorable, authentic, and deeply satisfying.
02
Weddings & Celebrations
From engagement dinners to wedding receptions, anniversaries, and milestone celebrations. Full-service catering with traditional Senegalese family-style service or plated presentations.
03
Cultural Events
Juneteenth celebrations, African Heritage Month dinners, DEI programming, and cultural festivals. Joloff's 30-year story adds meaning to every table we serve.
04
Schools, Churches & Nonprofits
Community institutions have been part of Joloff's story since 1990. We offer catering for community organizations, faith communities, schools, and nonprofits at accessible rates.
